## Distribution boundary

The release excludes raw/rendered media, customer material, generated run evidence, model weights,
Jianying/CapCut drafts, licensed BGM, credentials, login state and machine-local configuration.
The public repository starts from a new sanitized root commit. Existing private development
history, including historical machine paths, remains private and is not published or rewritten.

## Required human gates

- every final video requires complete human listening and viewing;
- factory production remains disabled until repeatability and human acceptance pass;
- case-study rendering remains Beta until its first formal deliverable is accepted;
- external models, Jianying entitlements and licensed BGM must be configured locally.

This document records a code/package pre-release, not a claim that all three workflows are fully
unattended or production-complete.

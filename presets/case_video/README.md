# Case Video preset

`case_video` is the independent preset for long-form customer case videos.
It does not inherit from or modify `video_diary` or `factory_demo`.

Primary output types:

- Standard case: 5–12 minutes.
- Extended case: 12–20 minutes.
- Derived short clips are downstream outputs, not the definition of a case.

The editorial source of truth is the verified spoken content and visible
factory evidence. Customer names, industries, figures and results must not be
invented or strengthened beyond the source.
